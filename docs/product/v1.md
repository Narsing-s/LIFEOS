# LIFEOS V1 + V1.5

## Goal

Deliver a privacy-first personal operating system around documents, universal search, personal memory, tasks/reminders, assets/warranties, finance, timeline, integrations and an AI life assistant.

## V1 foundation

- Account registration/login/session management
- Personal profile and preferences
- Responsive dashboard
- Document upload and processing foundation
- Universal search
- User-controlled memories
- Tasks and reminders
- Asset and warranty tracking
- AI assistant with retrieval and source citations
- PostgreSQL + pgvector, Redis/BullMQ and Docker
- OpenAPI and CI foundation

## V1.5 complete expansion

- Google OAuth and provider adapter foundation for Photos, Drive, Calendar, Gmail and Contacts
- Normalized life timeline
- Inbox normalization and triage
- Finance subscriptions, budgets and goals
- Notification delivery persistence and device registration foundation
- User-owned automation rules
- Cursor-based sync-run persistence
- Encrypted integration secrets
- Offline-first synchronization contracts for web/mobile
- Knowledge graph and memory-enrichment contracts
- Production security, observability and AI provider policy contracts

## Product loop

Capture -> Understand -> Connect -> Search -> Ask -> Plan -> Act -> Remember

## Acceptance examples

1. Connect Google with explicit scopes and keep credentials encrypted.
2. Import external events into the user's timeline without cross-user access.
3. Ask for a past event and retrieve related timeline, memories and records.
4. Create a budget/subscription/goal and surface it in the finance summary.
5. Create an automation with explicit conditions/actions and audit its execution.
6. Disconnect a provider and remove its connection without exposing its secrets.
